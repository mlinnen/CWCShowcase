## 2. Managing Events
This screen allows you to add, edit, or delete events.

### Adding a New Event
1. Click the **Add Icon (+)**.
2. Complete the required fields.
3. Click **Save**.

### Editing an Event
1. Click the row to open the **Detail View**.
2. Click the **Pencil Icon**.
3. Update the fields and click **Save**.
